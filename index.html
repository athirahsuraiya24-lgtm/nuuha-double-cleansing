YOUR CLEANSING RITUAL

Two simple steps.

Follow the NUUHA double cleansing routine for a gentle and thorough cleanse.

01

CLEANSING BALM

Melt & Emulsify

  1. Twist the bottom of the balm stick to dispense the product.
  2. Gently massage onto dry skin for 5–10 seconds.
  3. Once makeup and SPF begin to melt, add a little water to emulsify into a milky texture.
  4. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
02

FOAM CLEANSER

Lather & Cleanse

  1. Apply a small amount onto wet palms and lather until it foams.
  2. Massage onto the face using gentle circular motions.
  3. Rinse with lukewarm water and gently pat dry.
